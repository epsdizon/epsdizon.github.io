How a month runs

  1. CollectBank, card, Stripe and PayPal statements, plus receipts.
  2. CategorizeEvery transaction coded to your chart of accounts.
  3. ReconcileEach account matched to its statement, difference 0.00.
  4. QueryUnclear items parked in Suspense and sent to you as questions.
  5. ReportP&L and Balance Sheet with a short note on what changed.
